I've made this and I love it. It's a classic beef and bean chili that comes
together in one pot, and most of the time is just letting it simmer. It's even
better the next day.

The recipe comes from Holly at
[Spend With Pennies](https://www.spendwithpennies.com/the-best-chili-recipe/).
Here's how I make it.

## Ingredients

Makes 8 servings (about 1½ cups each).

- 2 pounds lean ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and finely diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2½ tablespoons chili powder, divided
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- 1 can (14.5 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 can (19 oz) red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, with the juice
- 1½ cups beef broth
- 1 cup beer
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar (optional)
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Steps

1. Mix the ground beef with **1½ tablespoons** of the chili powder.
2. Brown the beef in a large pot with the onion, jalapeño, and garlic. Drain
   the fat.
3. Add everything else and bring it to a boil.
4. Turn it down and simmer uncovered **45-60 minutes**, until it's as thick as
   you like it.
5. Top with cheddar, green onions, cilantro, or whatever you like.

## Macros

Estimated per bowl (about 1½ cups, 8 servings), without toppings:

| Calories | Protein | Carbs | Fat |
|---|---|---|---|
| ~300 | 27g | 19g (about 5g fiber) | 12g |

These are estimates from standard nutrition data for each ingredient, assuming
90/10 ground beef and counting the brown sugar and beer, so treat them as
ballpark numbers. Cheese and sour cream add up fast.

## Notes

- **No beer?** Use more beef broth instead.
- **Any ground meat works.** Turkey or a beef and pork mix are both fine.
- **Toppings:** sour cream, cheese, green or red onion, jalapeños, cilantro,
  avocado, lime, and tortilla chips.
