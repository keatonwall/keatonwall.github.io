I've made this and I love it. The sour cream makes it about the moistest banana
bread I've had, and the cinnamon sugar dusted in the pans gives the outside a
little crust. It makes three 9x5 loaves, so there's plenty to freeze or give
away.

The recipe comes from Esther Nelson on
[Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/6984/banana-sour-cream-bread/).
I leave out the walnuts and add 2 cups of chocolate chips, and I bake it in
three 9x5 pans instead of four small ones.

## Ingredients

Makes 3 (9x5-inch) loaves, about 3 cups of batter each, 30 slices. Each baked
loaf weighs about 900g.

- 3¼ cups white sugar, divided
- 3 teaspoons ground cinnamon, divided
- ¾ cup butter
- 6 very ripe bananas, mashed
- 1 container (16 oz) sour cream
- 3 eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 4½ cups all-purpose flour
- 3 teaspoons baking soda
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 cups chocolate chips

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to **300°F.** Grease three 9x5-inch loaf pans.
2. Mix **¼ cup** of the sugar with **1 teaspoon** of the cinnamon and dust the
   greased pans with it.
3. Beat the remaining 3 cups of sugar with the butter until smooth.
4. Add the bananas, sour cream, eggs, vanilla, and the remaining 2 teaspoons of
   cinnamon. Beat until well blended.
5. Add the flour, baking soda, and salt. Mix **just** until combined, then
   fold in the chocolate chips.
6. Divide the batter between the pans, roughly **3 cups** per loaf.
7. Bake **60 minutes** uncovered, then lay a sheet of foil over the top so it
   doesn't over-brown and bake about **15 minutes more** (75 minutes total),
   until a toothpick in the center comes out clean.

## Notes

- **Use really ripe bananas.** The spottier the better.
- **It freezes well.** Wrap a cooled loaf and freeze it.
- **Want nuts?** The original stirs in 1 cup of chopped walnuts at the end.
