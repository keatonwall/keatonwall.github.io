This is the chicken I keep coming back to. I've made it a bunch of times and
it's good every time. The trick is two seasoning packets from the grocery store
and a long soak.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb chicken tenderloins
- 1 packet **McCormick Grill Mates Mojito Lime** 15 Minute Marinade Mix
- 1 packet **Kroger Zesty Italian** Salad Dressing & Marinade Mix
- 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1/4 cup water

## Marinate

Mix both packets with the vinegar, oil, and water, and get the chicken coated.
Then let it marinate for **24 hours.**

The Mojito Lime packet says 15 minutes. Ignore that. The full day is what makes
this one.

## Cook

- Cook on the **gas grill, low and slow.**
- **Flip once,** about halfway through.
- Pull it when it hits **165F.** Go by temperature, not time. A probe
  thermometer makes it easy.
