I've made this and I love it. It's a buttery, caramel-like syrup that foams up
when you add the baking soda, and the foam is the best part. It goes on my
[buttermilk waffles](/blog/buttermilk-waffles), and it's great on French toast
or pancakes too.

The recipe comes from Katie Madrian on
[Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/239478/grandmas-buttermilk-syrup/).
I make it as written.

## Ingredients

Makes 6 servings.

- 1 cup white sugar
- ½ cup buttermilk
- ½ cup butter
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Steps

1. Heat the sugar, buttermilk, and butter in a saucepan over medium heat until
   it starts to boil, about **5 minutes.**
2. Take it off the heat and stir in the baking soda and vanilla.

## Notes

- **Use a bigger pan than you think.** It foams up a lot when the baking soda
  goes in.
- **Serve it right away.** The foam fades as it sits.
