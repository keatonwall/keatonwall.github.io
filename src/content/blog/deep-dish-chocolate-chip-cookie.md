I've made this and I love it. It's one big, thick cookie baked in a ramekin,
with golden edges and a soft center. It's the right amount of cookie when you
don't want a whole batch sitting around.

The recipe comes from Joanie at
[One Dish Kitchen](https://onedishkitchen.com/deep-dish-chocolate-chip-cookie-for-one/).
I make it as written.

## Ingredients

Makes 1 cookie, baked in a 10-ounce ramekin.

- 2 tablespoons salted butter, softened
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
- 1 large egg yolk
- ¼ teaspoon vanilla extract
- ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
- ⅛ teaspoon baking soda
- ⅛ teaspoon salt
- ¼ cup chocolate chips
- 2 tablespoons chopped pecans or walnuts

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to **350°F.**
2. Beat the butter and both sugars until light and fluffy, about **2 minutes.**
3. Beat in the egg yolk and vanilla.
4. Stir in the flour, baking soda, and salt **just** until the dough comes
   together.
5. Fold in the chocolate chips and nuts.
6. Butter a 10-ounce ramekin and press the dough in evenly.
7. Bake **22-25 minutes**, until the top is golden and the edges are set. The
   center will still look soft.
8. Let it cool **10 minutes** before digging in.

## Notes

- **Use a 10-ounce ramekin.** A wider dish bakes faster, so check it early.
- **Pull it while the middle looks soft.** The hot dish keeps baking it.
- **Good chocolate matters.** A chopped bar melts into bigger pools than chips.
- **Doubling it:** use two 10-ounce ramekins or one 5-inch square dish.
