I've made this and I love it. It's moist, heavy on the cinnamon, and makes two
loaves, so one can go in the freezer.

The recipe comes from Vicki Monte on
[Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/6698/moms-zucchini-bread/).
I make three changes: coconut oil instead of vegetable oil, 2 cups of sugar
instead of 2¼, and no walnuts.

## Ingredients

Makes 2 (8x4-inch) loaves, 24 slices.

- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 2 cups white sugar
- 1 cup coconut oil, melted
- 3 large eggs
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 2 cups shredded zucchini

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to **325°F.** Grease and flour two 8x4-inch loaf pans.
2. Sift the flour, cinnamon, salt, baking powder, and baking soda together.
3. In another bowl, beat the sugar, melted coconut oil, eggs, and vanilla until combined. Add
   the flour mixture and beat well.
4. Stir in the zucchini.
5. Pour the batter into the pans and bake **40-60 minutes**, until a toothpick
   in the center of each loaf comes out clean.
6. Cool in the pans **20 minutes**, then run a knife around the edges, turn
   them out onto a rack, and let them cool completely.

## Notes

- **It keeps.** It freezes well and lasts a long time in the fridge.
- **Want nuts?** The original stirs in 1 cup of chopped walnuts with the
  zucchini.
