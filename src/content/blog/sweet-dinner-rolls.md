I've made these and I love them. The bread machine does the mixing, kneading,
and first rise, then you roll them into crescents with butter spread inside.
They come out soft, a little sweet, and golden.

The recipe comes from Donna West on
[Allrecipes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/17690/sweet-dinner-rolls/).
The one change I make is using **3¼ cups** of flour instead of 3¾.

## Ingredients

Makes 16 rolls.

- ½ cup warm water (110°F)
- ½ cup warm milk
- ⅓ cup butter, softened
- 1 large egg
- 3¼ cups all-purpose flour
- ⅓ cup white sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 package (.25 oz) active dry yeast
- ¼ cup butter, softened, for spreading

## Steps

1. Put the water, milk, ⅓ cup butter, egg, flour, sugar, salt, and yeast in the
   bread machine in that order (or the order your machine calls for). Run the
   **Dough** cycle.
2. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured counter, cut it in half, and roll
   each half into a **12-inch circle.**
3. Spread the ¼ cup softened butter over both circles. Cut each into
   **8 wedges** and roll them up from the wide end, gently but tightly.
4. Set them point side down on an ungreased baking sheet, cover with a towel,
   and let them rise somewhere warm for **1 hour.**
5. Heat the oven to **400°F** and bake **10-15 minutes**, until golden.

## Notes

- **Flour:** I use 3¼ cups. The original calls for 3¾.
- **Cinnamon rolls:** the same dough works for cinnamon rolls.
