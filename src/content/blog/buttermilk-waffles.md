I've made these and I love them. They're light inside, crisp outside, and the
batter comes together in two bowls. Let it rest 10 minutes and they brown
better.

The recipe comes from Kristyn at
[Lil' Luna](https://lilluna.com/buttermilk-waffles/). I make it as written. I
top them with [Grandma's buttermilk syrup](/blog/grandmas-buttermilk-syrup).

## Ingredients

Makes 6 servings.

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 cups buttermilk, room temperature
- ⅓ cup butter, melted
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Steps

1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
2. In another bowl, whisk the buttermilk with the melted butter (let the butter
   cool a little first). Add the eggs and stir until combined.
3. Add the dry mix to the wet and whisk **just** until combined. A few small
   lumps should remain. Fold in the vanilla and let the batter rest
   **10 minutes.**
4. Heat and grease the waffle iron. Pour in enough batter to reach about ½ inch
   from the edge (usually ⅓ to ½ cup) and cook until golden and crisp. They're
   close to done when the steam slows way down.

## Notes

- **Don't overmix.** Leave the small lumps so they stay tender.
- **Keep them crisp:** hold finished waffles on a rack over a sheet pan in a
  225°F oven.
- **Make ahead:** the batter keeps covered in the fridge overnight. Let it come
  to room temperature and stir gently before cooking.
- **Leftovers:** 3-4 days in the fridge, or wrap each one and freeze for 2-3
  months.
