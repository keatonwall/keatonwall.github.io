I've made these and I love them. They're the cookies I'd make on a weeknight
because there's nothing to plan for: one bowl, melted butter, no mixer, and no
chilling the dough. You can go from "I want a cookie" to eating one in about 20
minutes.

The recipe comes from Kate at
[I Heart Eating](https://www.ihearteating.com/easiest-chocolate-chip-cookie-recipe/).
Here's how I make it.

## Ingredients

Makes 12 big cookies.

- ½ cup butter (salted or unsalted)
- ½ cup granulated sugar
- ¼ cup brown sugar, packed
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 large egg
- 1¾ cups all-purpose flour
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt (¼ teaspoon if you only have table salt)
- ½ cup **Lily's** semi-sweet baking chips
- ½ cup **Lily's** white chocolate baking chips

## Make the dough

1. Heat the oven to **350°F.**
2. Melt the butter in the microwave, about 40 seconds. You want it fully melted
   but not hot. If you're not sure, stop when it's about 90% melted and whisk
   until the rest goes.
3. Whisk the butter with both sugars until combined.
4. Stir in the vanilla and the egg.
5. Add the flour, baking soda, and salt. Mix **just** until it comes together.
   It should be soft and a little sticky.
6. Stir in the chips.

## Bake

Weigh out **68g** of dough per cookie (that's 12 big ones) and
space them 2 inches apart. Bake **9-11 minutes**, until the tops are slightly
golden brown.

## A few things that matter

- **Don't overdo the flour.** Weigh it if you can. If you're using cups, stir
  the flour first so it isn't packed. Too much flour is how you end up with dry
  cookies that don't spread.
- **Watch the salt.** It's ½ teaspoon of kosher salt. Table salt is saltier, so
  use half as much.
- **Want them chewier?** Flip the sugars: ½ cup brown, ¼ cup white.
- **Want them crispier?** Use all white sugar.
- **Mix-ins are fair game.** Milk chocolate, dark, white, or M&Ms all work.
