My plan for copying Culver's ButterBurgers at home on the Blackstone.

### The meat

Culver's uses a blend of three cuts: **sirloin, chuck, and plate** (short plate), fresh and never frozen. To match it, ask the butcher to grind roughly equal parts of each, aiming for about 20% fat. Short plate is the fatty one, so it's what gets you there. Fallback: 80/20 ground chuck.

### Ingredients

- Ground beef blend (sirloin, chuck, short plate), about 3 oz per patty
- Salt and pepper
- Butter, melted, for the buns
- Soft hamburger buns
- Pickles, sliced raw onion, ketchup, mustard
- American cheese (optional)

### Steps

1. Get the Blackstone very hot.
2. Loosely roll the beef into balls, about 3 oz each. Don't pack them.
3. Brush the cut sides of the buns with melted butter and toast them on the griddle until golden. The butter goes on the bun, not the meat.
4. Set the balls on the griddle and smash them thin right away. Salt and pepper.
5. Leave them alone until a dark crust forms, then flip once. Add cheese now if using.
6. Build with pickles, onion, ketchup, and mustard.
