Saved in case the original page goes away. Makes 8 servings (about 1½ cups each). 20 minutes of prep, 45 minutes of cooking.

### Ingredients

- 2 lb lean ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and finely diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2½ tablespoons chili powder, divided (adjust to taste)
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 green bell pepper, seeded and diced
- 1 can (14.5 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 can (19 oz) red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, with juices
- 1½ cups beef broth
- 1 cup beer
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar (optional)
- Salt and black pepper to taste

### Steps

1. Mix the ground beef with 1½ tablespoons of the chili powder.
2. In a large pot, brown the beef with the onion, jalapeño, and garlic. Drain off the fat.
3. Add the remaining ingredients and bring to a boil.
4. Lower the heat and simmer uncovered 45-60 minutes, until the chili is as thick as you like.
5. Serve topped with cheddar, green onions, cilantro, or other favorites.

**How I make it:** as written, no changes.

### Notes

- Serving size is about 1½ cups.
- Extra broth can replace the beer.
- Any ground meat works.
- Topping ideas: sour cream, red or green onion, cheese, jalapeños, cilantro, avocado, lime wedges, tortilla chips.
- The page lists about 395 calories, 29g protein, 27g carbs (7g fiber), and 17g fat per serving.
