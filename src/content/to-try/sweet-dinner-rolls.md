Saved in case the original page goes away. Makes 16 rolls. 20 minutes of prep, 1 hour 30 minutes of rising, 10-15 minutes in the oven.

### Ingredients

- ½ cup warm water (110°F)
- ½ cup warm milk
- ⅓ cup butter, softened
- 1 large egg
- 3¾ cups all-purpose flour
- ⅓ cup white sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 (.25 ounce) package active dry yeast
- ¼ cup butter, softened

### Steps

1. Place the water, milk, ⅓ cup butter, egg, flour, sugar, salt, and yeast in the bread machine pan in the order listed (or the order your manufacturer recommends). Select the Dough/Knead and First Rise cycle and press start.
2. When the cycle finishes, turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface. Divide it in half and roll each half into a 12-inch circle.
3. Spread the ¼ cup softened butter over each round. Cut each circle into 8 wedges and roll them from the wide end, gently but tightly. Place point side down on an ungreased cookie sheet. Cover with a clean towel and let rise in a warm place for 1 hour.
4. Meanwhile, preheat the oven to 400°F.
5. Bake until golden, 10 to 15 minutes.

**How I make it:** 3¼ cups of flour instead of 3¾. Everything else as written.

### Notes

- The same dough works for cinnamon rolls.
- The page lists about 192 calories, 4g protein, 27g carbs (1g fiber), and 8g fat per roll.
