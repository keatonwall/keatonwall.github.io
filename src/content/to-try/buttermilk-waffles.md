Saved in case the original page goes away. Makes 6 servings. 10 minutes of prep, 10 minutes of cooking.

### Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 2 cups buttermilk, room temperature
- ⅓ cup butter, melted
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

### Steps

1. In a medium bowl, whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
2. In another medium bowl, whisk the buttermilk and melted butter, letting the butter cool slightly first. Add the eggs and stir until combined.
3. Add the flour mixture to the wet ingredients and whisk until just combined (a few small lumps should remain). Fold in the vanilla. Let the batter rest 10 minutes.
4. Preheat and grease the waffle iron. Add enough batter to reach about ½ inch from the edge, usually ⅓ to ½ cup depending on the waffle maker. Cook until golden and crisp. The waffle is usually close to done when the steam slows significantly. Top with maple syrup, whipped cream, and berries.

**How I make it:** as written, no changes. I top them with [Grandma's buttermilk syrup](/blog/grandmas-buttermilk-syrup).

### Notes

- Don't overmix. Leave small lumps so the crumb stays tender.
- Resting the batter hydrates the flour and helps browning.
- Preheat the iron fully and grease lightly for crisp edges.
- Keep finished waffles warm and crisp on a rack over a sheet pan in a 225°F oven.
- Make ahead: the batter keeps covered in the fridge overnight. Bring it to room temperature and stir gently before using.
- Store: 3-4 days in the fridge in an airtight container, or wrap each waffle and freeze for 2-3 months.
