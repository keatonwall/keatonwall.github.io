Saved in case the original page goes away. Makes 2 (8x4-inch) loaves, 24 servings. 15 minutes of prep, 40-60 minutes in the oven, 20 minutes to cool.

### Ingredients

- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon, or to taste
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 2¼ cups white sugar
- 1 cup vegetable oil
- 3 large eggs
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 2 cups shredded zucchini
- 1 cup chopped walnuts

### Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 325°F. Grease and flour two 8x4-inch loaf pans.
2. Sift the flour, cinnamon, salt, baking powder, and baking soda together in a large bowl.
3. In a separate large bowl, beat the sugar, oil, eggs, and vanilla with an electric mixer until combined. Add the flour mixture and beat well.
4. Stir in the zucchini and walnuts until well combined.
5. Pour the batter into the prepared pans.
6. Bake until a toothpick in the center of each loaf comes out clean, 40 to 60 minutes. Cool in the pans on a wire rack 20 minutes.
7. Run a table knife around the edges, invert onto a wire rack, and let cool completely.

**How I make it:** coconut oil instead of vegetable oil, 2 cups of sugar instead of 2¼, and no walnuts.

### Notes

- Freezes well and keeps in the fridge for weeks.
- The page lists about 256 calories, 3g protein, 32g carbs (1g fiber), and 13g fat per serving.
