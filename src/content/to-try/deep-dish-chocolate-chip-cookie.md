Saved in case the original page goes away. Makes 1 serving in a 10-ounce ramekin. 5 minutes of prep, 25 minutes in the oven, 10 minutes to cool.

**Oven:** 350°F.

### Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons salted butter, softened to room temperature
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
- 1 large egg yolk
- ¼ teaspoon vanilla extract
- ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
- ⅛ teaspoon baking soda
- ⅛ teaspoon salt
- ¼ cup chocolate chips
- 2 tablespoons chopped pecans or walnuts

### Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. In a small bowl, beat the softened butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar until light and fluffy, about 2 minutes.
3. Beat in the egg yolk and vanilla until smooth.
4. Add the flour, baking soda, and salt and stir just until the dough comes together.
5. Fold in the chocolate chips and the nuts, if using.
6. Butter a 10-ounce ramekin and press the dough into it in an even layer.
7. Bake 22 to 25 minutes, until the top is golden and the edges are set. The center will still look soft.
8. Cool on a wire rack 10 minutes before serving.

**How I make it:** as written, no changes.

### Notes

- Use a 10-ounce ramekin. A wider or larger dish bakes shallower and faster, so start checking several minutes early. A smaller one may not hold all the dough, so split it between two.
- Cream the butter until light but still cool. Butter that's too warm won't hold air.
- Take it out while the center still looks soft. The hot dish keeps baking it as it cools.
- Use good chocolate. Chopped chocolate or chunks melt into bigger pools than chips.
- To double it, bake in two 10-ounce ramekins or one 5-inch square baking dish.
