Saved in case the original page goes away. One bowl, no mixer, no chilling. Makes about 24 cookies in under 20 minutes (10 minutes of prep, 7-10 in the oven).

**Oven:** 350°F.

### Ingredients

- ½ cup butter (salted or unsalted)
- ½ cup granulated sugar
- ¼ cup brown sugar, packed
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 large egg
- 1¾ cups all-purpose flour, measured carefully
- ½ teaspoon baking soda
- ½ teaspoon kosher salt (¼ teaspoon if using table salt)
- 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips

### Steps

1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. Melt the butter in the microwave, about 40 seconds. It should be fully melted but not hot or sizzling. If you're unsure, stop at about 90% melted and whisk until the rest melts.
3. In a large bowl, whisk the butter and both sugars until well combined.
4. Stir in the vanilla and egg.
5. Add the flour, baking soda, and salt and mix just until combined. The dough should be soft and a little sticky, but not overly sticky.
6. Stir in the chocolate chips.
7. Scoop about 1½ tablespoons of dough per cookie (a medium cookie scoop) and set them 2 inches apart on a baking sheet.
8. Bake 7-10 minutes, until the edges are set and the middles still look puffy and a little underdone. Pulling them early keeps them soft.

**How I bake them:** 9-11 minutes, until the tops are slightly golden brown.

### Notes

- **Flour:** weigh it if you can. If you're using cups, stir or sift the flour first. Too much flour makes dry, crumbly dough and cookies that don't spread.
- **Chewier:** swap the sugars (½ cup brown, ¼ cup granulated).
- **Crispier:** use only granulated sugar. Dark brown sugar gives a slightly richer flavor than light.
- **Chips:** semisweet is the default. Milk, dark, white, or M&Ms all work.
