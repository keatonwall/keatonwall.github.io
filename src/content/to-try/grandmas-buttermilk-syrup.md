Saved in case the original page goes away. Makes 6 servings. 5 minutes of prep, 5 minutes of cooking.

### Ingredients

- 1 cup white sugar
- ½ cup buttermilk
- ½ cup butter
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

### Steps

1. Heat the sugar, buttermilk, and butter in a small saucepan over medium heat until the mixture starts to boil, about 5 minutes.
2. Remove from the heat and stir in the baking soda and vanilla until incorporated.

**How I make it:** as written, no changes. It goes on my [buttermilk waffles](/blog/buttermilk-waffles).

### Notes

- Use a pan big enough for the syrup to foam up.
- Serve it right away. The foam slowly disappears, and it's the best part.
- The page lists about 275 calories, 1g protein, 34g carbs, and 16g fat per serving.
